What is Among us? We are a student founded startup that focuses on solving the problem of having to buy equipments once that will be used once and never again. Equipments like drawing kits, dissection kits can cost thousands and are used once then kept in the student's drawer after with no use afterward. We plan on buying on such equipments and rent them to students who need it for the amount of time they need it for at a fraction of the original cost.
How do we work? For accessing our services, you need to login first.You can contact our helpline in case of any issues. Once you login, you can then select an equipment, choose an available time period for that and submit it. Also note that you need to request for an equipment atleast 2 hours before the time of delivery. Then you can recieve your equipment and have to pay for it. This is a small summary of the process.
