A detailed explanation of the rental process.

Making an account

First step is to log in. Use the Login button on top for that. It will ask for your Name, the name of your college and a clear photo of your ID card. Please note we do not provide services to all colleges now, you can only use our services if your college is there in the dropbox that can be seen during the login process. You can then set a nickname and password that can be used to login the next time.

Booking and getting your equipment

You can browse our website and look for the equipment you require. Once you find it, you can set the amount of that equipment. You also need to choose a time slot at which we will deliver the equipments to a place that will be specified before. You can recieve your item and have to pay for the rental. Make sure to check your equipment before paying. Any complaints stating that the given equipment was damaged will not be accepted. You can also extend the time of keeping the equipment through the website itself given that the initially specified time slot is not over.

Returning the equipment

Once the specified time is done, you need to return the equipment back. Generally the location of returning will be the same as the location of receiving your equipment.If the equipment is damaged while usage, you will have to pay the full amount for replacing the equipment. Please note that ANY DELAY IN RETURNING THE EQUIPMENT WILL COST YOU EXTRA. Extra cost will be calculated based on the extra time you had it for. If it exceeds 24 hours, there will be an extra late charge on top of the extra fees. Repeated failure in returning the equipment on time can cause a temporary suspension or a permanent ban from the platform.
